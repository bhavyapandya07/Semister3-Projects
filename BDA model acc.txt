Model Accuracy: RMSE = 1.3086
What does RMSE mean?
RMSE = Root Mean Square Error — it measures how far off the model's predictions are from reality, on average.

Our interaction scores range from 1.0 to 5.0:

Event	Score
view	1.0
addtocart	3.0
transaction	5.0
With RMSE = 1.3086, the model's prediction is off by ±1.31 points on average.

Is 1.3086 good or bad?
It's acceptable and expected for this type of data. Here's why:

❌ Don't compare it to Netflix-style star ratings (1–5 stars).
✅ This is implicit feedback data — users never explicitly said "I rate this product 4/5". We're inferring preferences from behaviour (clicks, carts, purchases).

Implicit feedback data is inherently noisy:

A user might view a product 10 times but never buy it (window shopping)
A user might buy something as a gift (not their own taste)
This noise means RMSE will naturally be higher than explicit rating datasets
For implicit feedback ALS on e-commerce data, RMSE between 1.0–2.0 is the typical industry range. We're well within that. ✅
